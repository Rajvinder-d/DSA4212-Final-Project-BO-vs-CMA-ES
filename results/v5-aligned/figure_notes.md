# V5 figure definitions

19 complete initial-design groups; identity orientation, five-dimensional ball, deterministic DST calibration loss. Every arm has 20 paid initial observations and 300 total calls. CMA has 5 search replicates per design and handler; BO/random have one.

`quality_and_time.png`: the calls panel takes the mean RMSE curve of replicates within each design, then the median across designs. The time panel displays individual trajectories without an aggregate time ranking. Search time excludes compilation/setup and post-search FUDS assessment.

`handler_contrast.png`: mean log10 loss ratios after averaging replicates within designs; percentile intervals resample whole designs. The 100-call comparison is primary, 300 calls secondary.
